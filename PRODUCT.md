# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Women 18–55 in Lviv booking laser epilation, facial care, or massage. Two main arrival paths: first-timers comparing local studios, and visitors coming from Skinera's Instagram (mostly on phones). The site's job for them: understand what Skinera offers, trust it, and book.

## Product Purpose

Skinera is a beauty studio in Lviv offering laser epilation, professional facial care, and massage. This app exists to turn a visitor into a booked appointment or consultation. Success = bookings from first-time visitors.

## Positioning

A combination neighbors rarely hold together: unhurried, individual care in a calm studio; experienced certified masters; certified equipment; accessible pricing with first-visit offers. No single claim is unique — the combination and the calm, unrushed experience are the position.

## Operating Context

- Many visitors arrive from Instagram, mostly on mobile.
- Comparison shopping across Lviv studios: price, trust signals, convenience.
- Primary action: «Записатися» (booking or consultation); phone is a secondary channel.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Scope of this app: salon only — home page and service pages (laser epilation, facial care, massage), contacts, booking.
- Out of scope: the cosmetics shop (catalog, favorites, cart, checkout) and user accounts. Code for them still exists in the repo but is not part of this app's product direction. (If a shop returns later, its audience is buyers anywhere in Ukraine via Nova Poshta.)
- Services: laser epilation (zones: face, underarms, bikini, legs, arms, back); facial care (cleansing, peels, facial massage); massage (relax, lymphatic drainage, back, body hardware massage).
- Language: Ukrainian (`lang="uk"`); all user-facing copy in Ukrainian.
- Undecided: booking mechanism (currently a callback form in a modal), real phone number, final prices and offer terms.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Skinera. Logo: `public/icon/logo-ink.png`.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real studio media: hero photo + procedure montage video in `public/media/` (`hero-*`, `hero2-montage.*`), service photos `public/media/svc-{laser,facial,massage}4.jpg`.
- **Prices are placeholders** (laser від 450 ₴, facial/massage від 700 ₴) and stay visibly marked with `*` until real prices are supplied.
- No real ratings, review counts, years of experience, procedure counts, or testimonials are on hand. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Booking is the point — every surface shortens the path to an appointment.
2. Calm over pressure — the promise of unhurried care must be felt, not just stated.
3. Trust through truth — only real proof; placeholders stay clearly placeholders.
4. Mobile-first reality — Instagram traffic lands on phones.
